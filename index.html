01 · PROSPECTING

Lead Finder

Automated prospecting for cold outreach to service businesses – salons, studios, clinics – across DACH.

  • Finds businesses via Google Maps by industry, city and radius; sync only adds new ones. Plus Instagram search via the official Meta API
  • Checks every website and detects 20+ booking systems, chains, new openings and pain points from reviews
  • Booking-click via scraping: automatically clicks the booking button on the website and the Google profile and shows what comes next – booking tool, WhatsApp, phone only or contact form; this also flags existing customers. Plus owner name and phone number
  • Scores every lead 0–100% with A/B/C tiers, so the best ones get called first
  • Interactive map: every lead as clusters (colour = best tier) or a heatmap, the same filters as the tables, click a point to see everything about the lead
  • Deep check: an individual cheat sheet for every call
  • Team features: assign leads to colleagues (own list per person), an “open now” filter for the right time to call, HubSpot/Excel export, undo for every change
  • 5,000+ qualified leads so far
  • Python
  • Flask
  • SQLite
  • Google Places API

03 · GROWTH

Content Intelligence

A private project for micro-influencer friends: it pulls their Instagram data together, shows what works and tells them what to post next. A real-estate broker also uses it for its content.

  • Profile check: followers, engagement rate, posts per week, reel share – and every post's impact relative to the profile median (lift) by format, topic, weekday and caption length
  • Top and flop posts with the reasons why, turned into a verdict and 3 to-dos for this week
  • Weekly plan: format and posting time per day, what to shoot and hooks to test from a hook library (1,761 analysed posts)
  • Reel analysis with a re-shoot recipe, profile comparison with competitors, before/after
