01 · PROSPECTING

Lead Finder

Automated prospecting for cold outreach to service businesses – salons, studios, clinics – across DACH.

  • Finds businesses via Google Maps by industry, city and radius; sync only adds new ones
  • Checks every website and detects 20+ booking systems, chains, new openings and pain points from reviews
  • Scores every lead 0–100% with A/B/C tiers, so the best ones get called first
  • Team features: assignment, status tracking, CRM/Excel export, undo for every change
  • Python
  • Flask
  • SQLite
  • Google Places API
